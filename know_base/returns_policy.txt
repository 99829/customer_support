RETURNS & CANCELLATION POLICY

1. RETURN WINDOW
Most products can be returned within 7 days of delivery. Electronics have a 10-day return window. Perishable items, innerwear, and customized products are non-returnable.

2. RETURN CONDITIONS
Items must be unused, in original packaging, with all tags/accessories intact. Defective or damaged items are exempt from these conditions and are eligible for return regardless of usage.

3. CANCELLATION POLICY
Orders can be cancelled free of charge before they are shipped. Once an order status changes to "Shipped," cancellation is no longer possible - customers must wait for delivery and then initiate a return instead.

4. REFUND FOR CANCELLED ORDERS
If an order is cancelled before shipping, the full amount is refunded within 3-5 business days. If payment was made via COD, no refund is needed since no payment was collected.

5. EXCHANGE POLICY
Size or color exchanges are allowed within 7 days of delivery, subject to stock availability. Exchanges do not require a refund - the new item is shipped once the original is picked up.
