PAYMENT & REFUND POLICY

1. PAYMENT METHODS
We accept UPI, Credit/Debit Cards, Net Banking, and Cash on Delivery (COD). COD is available for orders under Rs. 5,000 only.

2. FAILED PAYMENTS WITH MONEY DEDUCTED
If a payment shows "Failed" but money was deducted from your account, this is usually a bank processing delay. The amount is automatically reversed to your original payment method within 5-7 business days. If not reversed within 7 days, contact support with your transaction ID for manual intervention.

3. REFUND TIMELINES
Refunds for cancelled or returned orders are processed within 3-5 business days after approval. The amount reflects in your account within 5-7 additional business days depending on your bank, for UPI/card payments. COD order refunds are processed via bank transfer and require the customer to submit bank details.

4. PARTIAL PAYMENTS
Partial payments are not supported. All orders must be paid in full at checkout, except for orders using "Pay Later" options where available.

5. PAYMENT DISPUTES
For any payment discrepancy (double charge, incorrect amount charged), customers should raise a dispute within 30 days of the transaction. Disputes are investigated within 5 business days.
