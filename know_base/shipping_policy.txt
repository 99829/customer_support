SHIPPING & DELIVERY POLICY

1. DELIVERY TIMELINES
Standard delivery takes 5-7 business days from order confirmation. Express delivery (available in select cities) takes 2-3 business days. Delivery timelines may be extended by 2-3 days during sale periods or due to weather/logistics disruptions.

2. DELAYED DELIVERY
If your order is delayed beyond the expected delivery date by more than 3 days, you are eligible for a delivery fee waiver. If delayed beyond 7 days past the expected date, customers can request a full refund or replacement, whichever they prefer.

3. TRACKING
All orders come with real-time tracking available in the "My Orders" section. Tracking updates may take 24-48 hours to reflect after dispatch.

4. WRONG/DAMAGED ITEM DELIVERED
If you receive a wrong or damaged item, report it within 48 hours of delivery through the app with photo evidence. A replacement or full refund will be processed within 5-7 business days after verification.

5. FAILED DELIVERY ATTEMPTS
If delivery fails due to customer unavailability, 2 more attempts will be made over the next 3 days. After 3 failed attempts, the order is returned to the seller and a refund is initiated automatically.
